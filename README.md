# VoidEQ

### System-wide Audio EQ & DSP for Android

VoidEQ gives you control over the way your Android device sounds.

Built for people who want more from their audio, VoidEQ combines a system-wide equalizer with powerful audio effects, presets, and a clean interface.

### Latest Release

## Features

- System-wide audio equalizer
- Custom frequency control
- Bass enhancement
- Reverb
- Spatial audio
- Virtualizer
- Audio presets
- Bluetooth audio support
- Clean AMOLED-friendly interface
- Simple and responsive controls

## Android Compatibility

**Android 7.0 and above**

VoidEQ is designed to work across a wide range of Android devices.

Audio behavior can vary between manufacturers because Android audio implementations differ between devices.

VoidEQ is being tested on devices from manufacturers including:

- Samsung
- Xiaomi
- OnePlus
- Stock Android

## Installation

1. Download the latest APK from [Releases](../../releases).
2. Open the APK on your Android device.
3. Install VoidEQ.
4. Open the app.
5. Start the audio engine.
6. Configure your EQ and effects.
7. Play your music and enjoy.

---

## Why VoidEQ?

I built VoidEQ because finding a good Android equalizer with powerful audio controls without having to pay for basic functionality was frustrating.

So I decided to build my own.

The goal was simple:

> Give Android users more control over their audio.

VoidEQ is built around that idea.

## Feedback & Bug Reports

If you find something that doesn't work correctly, please report it.

When reporting an audio issue, include:

- Device model
- Android version
- Audio output being used
- EQ/effects currently enabled
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened

This makes it much easier to investigate device-specific issues.
